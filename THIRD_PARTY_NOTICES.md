# Third-party notices / 第三方声明

This repository includes a pinned, normalized snapshot of third-party academic skills. Their source instructions and assets are preserved under `skills/`; LL-AcademicSkillsHub adds catalog metadata and bilingual usage profiles.

本仓库包含经过固定版本与目录规范化处理的第三方学术技能快照。其原始指令与资源保留在 `skills/` 中；链邻新增的是目录元数据和双语使用说明。

- The legacy bundle metadata declares the collection as MIT-licensed, but a collection-level declaration does not automatically settle every nested file, dataset, model, API, or runtime dependency.
- Records marked `metadata-declared` are redistributable candidates whose per-skill provenance still needs confirmation before a downstream commercial release.
- Records marked `review-required` must not be represented as license-cleared.
- Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
- No third-party skill is represented as authored, endorsed, or verified by Lianlin unless the record explicitly says `lianlin-first-party`.

The generated audit report is the authoritative review snapshot for this release.
