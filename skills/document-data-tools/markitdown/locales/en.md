# Markitdown

Convert files and office documents to Markdown. Supports PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, images (with OCR), audio (with transcription), HTML, CSV, JSON, XML, ZIP, YouTube URLs, EPubs and more.

## Main capabilities

- Read and transform research documents
- Extract structured content
- Preserve layout, fields, and provenance

## Inputs

- Documents, spreadsheets, or data files

## Outputs

- Converted files, structured data, or validation reports
