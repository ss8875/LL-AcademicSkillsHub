# Bgpt Paper Search

Search scientific papers and retrieve structured experimental data extracted from full-text studies via the BGPT MCP server. Returns 25+ fields per paper including methods, results, sample sizes, quality scores, and conclusions. Use for literature reviews, evidence synthesis, and finding experimental details not available in abstracts alone.

## Main capabilities

- Search and screen scholarly literature
- Organize metadata and citations
- Preserve auditable search evidence

## Inputs

- Research question, keywords, and search limits

## Outputs

- Literature records, screening results, or citation data
