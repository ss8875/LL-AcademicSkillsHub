# Literature Review

Conduct comprehensive, systematic literature reviews using multiple academic databases (PubMed, arXiv, bioRxiv, Semantic Scholar, etc.). This skill should be used when conducting systematic literature reviews, meta-analyses, research synthesis, or comprehensive literature searches across biomedical, scientific, and technical domains. Creates professionally formatted markdown documents and PDFs with verified citations in multiple citation styles (APA, Nature, Vancouver, etc.).

## Main capabilities

- Search and screen scholarly literature
- Organize metadata and citations
- Preserve auditable search evidence

## Inputs

- Research question, keywords, and search limits

## Outputs

- Literature records, screening results, or citation data
