# Pdb Database

Access RCSB PDB for 3D protein/nucleic acid structures. Search by text/sequence/structure, download coordinates (PDB/mmCIF), retrieve metadata, for structural biology and drug discovery.

## Main capabilities

- Analyze protein sequences and structures
- Run structural-biology workflows
- Assess design results and uncertainty

## Inputs

- Protein sequences, structures, or design goals

## Outputs

- Structures, scores, annotations, or design candidates
