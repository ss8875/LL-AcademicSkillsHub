# Lianlin Bilingual Full-Text Evidence Reader

Align terminology across Chinese and English full texts, anchor evidence, compare methods, and label uncertainty.

## Main capabilities

- Confirm text versions, languages, and available scope
- Build a bilingual terminology map retaining source terms
- Extract evidence for questions, methods, data, results, and limits
- Record page or structural anchors for facts and quotations
- Separate literal, contextual, conventional, and non-equivalent terms
- Produce a bilingual evidence matrix and discrepancy notes

## Inputs

- Chinese and English full texts or lawful excerpts and a research question

## Outputs

- Bilingual glossary, evidence matrix, and discrepancy report
