# Lianlin Paper Evidence Analysis

Build a traceable paper evidence card spanning question, theory, methods, data, results, novelty, and limitations.

## Main capabilities

- Record paper identity, version, and acquisition route
- Separate author claims, paper evidence, and analyst inference
- Extract design, sample, variables, methods, and main results
- Anchor each key claim to a page, figure, table, or section
- Assess internal, external, statistical, and reporting validity
- Produce a one-page evidence card with cross-paper fields

## Inputs

- Full paper or lawfully available text and analysis question

## Outputs

- Paper evidence card with anchors and quality appraisal
