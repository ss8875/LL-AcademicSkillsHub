# Lianlin Research Data Availability & FAIR

Review research data for findability, accessibility, interoperability, and reuse, then draft a data-availability statement.

## Main capabilities

- Inventory datasets, code, materials, and derived results
- Confirm privacy, ethics, contract, IP, and embargo constraints
- Choose a domain repository and plan persistent identifiers and versions
- Check metadata, formats, dictionaries, licenses, and reproduction notes
- Assess FAIR gaps and prioritized remediations
- Draft a statement consistent with actual access conditions

## Inputs

- Data asset inventory, policy requirements, and access constraints

## Outputs

- FAIR checklist, deposit plan, and data-availability statement
