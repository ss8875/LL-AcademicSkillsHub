# Treatment Plans

Generate concise (3-4 page), focused medical treatment plans in LaTeX/PDF format for all clinical specialties. Supports general medical treatment, rehabilitation therapy, mental health care, chronic disease management, perioperative care, and pain management. Includes SMART goal frameworks, evidence-based interventions with minimal text citations, regulatory compliance (HIPAA), and professional formatting. Prioritizes brevity and clinical actionability.

## Main capabilities

- Organize clinical and precision-medicine evidence
- Handle controlled medical data
- State applicability limits and risks

## Inputs

- Clinical question, medical data, or evidence material

## Outputs

- Evidence summaries, analysis results, or structured records
