# Datamol

Pythonic wrapper around RDKit with simplified interface and sensible defaults. Preferred for standard drug discovery including SMILES parsing, standardization, descriptors, fingerprints, clustering, 3D conformers, parallel processing. Returns native rdkit.Chem.Mol objects. For advanced control or custom parameters, use rdkit directly.

## Main capabilities

- Analyze chemical structures and properties
- Query chemistry and drug databases
- Support candidate screening and assessment

## Inputs

- Molecular structures, compound identifiers, or screening criteria

## Outputs

- Structure data, property predictions, or candidate lists
