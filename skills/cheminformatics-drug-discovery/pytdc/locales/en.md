# Pytdc

Therapeutics Data Commons. AI-ready drug discovery datasets (ADME, toxicity, DTI), benchmarks, scaffold splits, molecular oracles, for therapeutic ML and pharmacological prediction.

## Main capabilities

- Analyze chemical structures and properties
- Query chemistry and drug databases
- Support candidate screening and assessment

## Inputs

- Molecular structures, compound identifiers, or screening criteria

## Outputs

- Structure data, property predictions, or candidate lists
