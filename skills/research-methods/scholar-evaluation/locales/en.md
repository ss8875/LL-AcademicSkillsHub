# Scholar Evaluation

Systematically evaluate scholarly work using the ScholarEval framework, providing structured assessment across research quality dimensions including problem formulation, methodology, analysis, and writing with quantitative scoring and actionable feedback.

## Main capabilities

- Clarify questions and hypotheses
- Design reproducible research workflows
- Identify bias, limitations, and validation points

## Inputs

- Research question, data conditions, and methodological constraints

## Outputs

- Study design, analysis plan, or methodological assessment
