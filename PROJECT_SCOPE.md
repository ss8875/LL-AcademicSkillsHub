# Project Scope

## Brand

- Project: `LL-AcademicSkillsHub`
- Chinese name: `链邻学术技能仓库`
- Product companion: `链邻科研AI平台`

## Initial release

The initial release is local-first and bilingual (`zh-CN`, `en`).

Supported user routes:

1. deploy the repository locally;
2. download the Lianlin Research AI Platform.

## Non-goals

- No external agent compatibility promise.
- No cloud SaaS.
- No public multi-user account system.
- No automatic installation of every scientific dependency.
- No CAPTCHA or access-control bypass.
- No redistribution of content without a recorded license basis.
- No “whole web” or “most complete on the internet” claim.
- No third-party platform advertising skill.

## Brand architecture

Public-facing architecture begins with LL-AcademicSkillsHub itself. Source discovery, licensing, and contribution review are internal governance processes rather than a public brand layer.

## Language policy

- Simplified Chinese and English ship together.
- Human-facing catalog content must be available in both languages.
- First-party Lianlin core skills require full bilingual instructions.
- Pinned third-party skills retain their source instructions and receive bilingual catalog metadata and local usage profiles.
- Translation parity is validated automatically.

## Licensing policy

- First-party Lianlin code, skills, documentation, catalog metadata, website code, and original brand assets are source-available under PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 from 2026-07-26.
- Commercial use is not granted by default and requires separate prior written authorization from the applicable copyright holder.
- Third-party materials remain under their upstream terms and are never relicensed merely by inclusion.
- A Lianlin commercial authorization does not clear third-party rights.
- Historical first-party versions already received under Apache-2.0 remain governed by that prior grant; the current license change is prospective, not retroactive.
