# LL-AcademicSkillsHub brand

- English name: **LL-AcademicSkillsHub**
- Chinese name: **链邻学术技能仓库**
- Positioning: a locally deployable, bilingual, auditable academic skill repository.
- Primary promise: discover, understand, install, and review academic skills from one structured catalog.
- Assisted route: users who do not want to install individual skills can use the **Lianlin Research AI Platform / 链邻科研 AI 平台**.

## Public wording boundary

Use “comprehensive academic skill hub” or “系统化学术技能仓库”. Do not claim “all skills on the internet”, universal compatibility, universal verification, or ownership of vendored third-party work.

Platform promotion belongs in the README, documentation site, download page, and release notes. It must not be embedded as an always-running skill or interrupt a research workflow.
